FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can I change my plan later?

Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade your membership at any time from your member dashboard.

Do I need to book group classes in advance?

We recommend booking through the Classes page so your spot is guaranteed, especially for popular evening sessions.

How do personal training sessions work?

Pro and Elite members get PT sessions included. Your trainer builds a plan around your goals and tracks your progress.

Is the BMI calculator medical advice?

No. BMI is a general screening tool. Speak to a qualified professional for personalised health guidance.
